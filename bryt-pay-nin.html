BRYT Pay · NIN Slip

Your NIN slip for ₦300, sold by doing the maths on screen.

ClientBRYT Pay
SectorFintech · Nigeria
TypeFeature ad
Year2026
The brief

Push one feature hard: getting your NIN slip on BRYT Pay for ₦300, instead of a trip to the cyber café.

The approach

Open on the pain everyone knows: ₦1,000 at the café, transport and a queue. Then flip to the phone, where the slip is ready in minutes. It closes on a side-by-side price comparison, "Same slip. Way cheaper.", so the saving is impossible to miss.
